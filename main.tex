\chapter*{Editorial note}
This independent English study edition reconstructs the handwritten-design questions from the supplied Academic Year 112 midterm and final examinations. Each question is followed by a worked solution and editable circuit drawings. Supplied answers are fallible references, not an authoritative answer key. Original question scans, photographs, and reference-answer files are not published.

Academic Year 112 corresponds to 2023--2024 in the local academic calendar; the supplied exam sheets do not print exact dates. The midterm's printed weights total 140\%, and are preserved. The final is one 100\% controller-design question. No simulation-software-only question was found in these two exam sheets. Unspecified conventions and additional reset assumptions are stated explicitly.
